FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know Before Ordering

Have questions before we get started? Here are straightforward answers to our most common client inquiries.

For standard flyers, posters, and social media graphics, turnaround is typically 24 to 48 hours after we receive your brief and content. Full brand identity suites and custom logo packages take between 3 to 7 business days. If you are on an urgent deadline, same-day express delivery (12 hours) is available upon request.

To ensure a fast and accurate design, prepare:
1. Complete text details (Event name, theme, date, time, venue, guest ministers/speakers, contact info).
2. High-resolution photos of speakers, hosts, or products.
3. High-resolution logo file (preferably PNG with transparent background or vector).
4. Any specific color preferences or reference flyer examples you admire.

We typically require a 50% commitment deposit to commence design work, with the remaining 50% balance due upon your final design approval right before source and high-resolution print files are delivered. We accept Mobile Money, Bank Transfers, PayPal, and major credit cards.

CLIENT AGREEMENT

Terms of Service

Project Policies & Creative Deliverables Standards

1. Studio Engagement

By commissioning Addotei Creative Studio (led by Creative Director David Addotei), you agree to these standard project terms. Work begins upon receipt of a confirmed project brief and commitment deposit.

2. Deposit & Payment Schedule

  • A 50% commitment deposit is required upfront before design drafting and concept exploration begins.
  • The remaining 50% balance is payable upon your approval of the final watermark proof, prior to releasing unwatermarked 300 DPI master print and web files.
  • Accepted channels include MTN Mobile Money, Telecel Cash, Direct Bank Transfer, and PayPal.

3. Revision Policy

Every standard design package includes up to 2 to 3 rounds of revisions (text changes, color tweaks, element sizing, speaker photo replacements). Drastic scope changes requested after initial brief approval (e.g. changing the entire event concept, date, or new theme) are billed as a new design.

4. Production Turnaround

Standard turnaround is 24 to 48 hours from deposit clearance and asset receipt (high-res photos, approved text). Delays in client feedback or missing content may extend production timelines.

5. Cancellations & Refunds

Because creative design reserves dedicated studio labor and hours, deposits are non-refundable once initial concept drafting has commenced.
